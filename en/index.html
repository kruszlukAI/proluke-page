Specialty Industrial Chemicals

Certified inorganic minerals and high-purity chemical compounds with reproducible specifications, including products from the Swedish manufacturer Alufluor AB.

Al(OH)3

Dried Aluminium Hydroxide

High-purity dried aluminium hydroxide (ATH) with uniform particle sizing and minimal moisture. Essential precursor for synthesis and halogen-free mineral flame retardant.

  • Water treatment coagulants manufacturing (PAC, aluminium sulphate)
  • Eco-friendly flame retardant for plastics, cables, and elastomers
  • Raw material for technical glass, ceramics, and zeolites
Request Technical Data →
SiO2

Silicon Dioxide (Silica)

Synthetic amorphous silicon dioxide characterized by high specific surface area and controlled purity. Superior functional carrier and rheology modifier.

  • Anti-caking agent and active carrier for technical chemicals and feeds
  • Reinforcing mineral filler in elastomer and polymer compound systems
  • Viscosity and rheology control in paints, coatings, adhesives, and sealants
Request Technical Data →
AlF3

Aluminium Fluoride

Anhydrous aluminium fluoride of premier chemical purity from Alufluor AB. Indispensable flux reducing electrolyte melting point and energy consumption in smelting.

  • Primary aluminium smelting bath conditioning and operational efficiency
  • Specialty optical glass, laser optics, and technical ceramics
  • Non-ferrous metallurgy flux and brazing formulations
Request Technical Data →
CaF2

Calcium Fluoride (Synthetic Fluorspar)

Synthetic calcium fluoride offering homogeneous mineral composition and negligible trace impurities. A pure, reliable alternative to natural mined fluorspar.

  • Slag conditioning flux in secondary steelmaking and foundry casting
  • Welding electrode flux coatings and submerged arc welding powders
  • Glass manufacturing, specialty enamels, and ceramics production
